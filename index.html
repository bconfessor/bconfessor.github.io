About

Computer engineer specializing in Unity and XR development.

I am a PhD candidate at the Nara Institute of Science and Technology and a Unity XR engineer at Premium Arts Inc. My work covers gameplay systems, UI programming and AR/VR applications. My research examines how video games can support the resocialization of people at risk of social isolation.

I completed my Master's degree in the same laboratory in 2024. Before that, I worked on VR education software, maritime simulation and enterprise systems in Brazil.
